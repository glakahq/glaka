# Services and prices

All prices include labour and VAT. Materials (tiles, suites, fittings) are quoted separately unless stated.

## Full bathroom refit

Strip-out, plumbing, electrics to the extractor and lighting, tiling, and fitting of a supplied suite. Typical duration is 7 to 10 working days.

From £4,800 for a standard bathroom up to 2.5m x 2m. Larger rooms are quoted after a survey.

## Shower installation

Electric shower fitted to an existing supply: £450.
Mixer shower with new pipework: from £850.
Walk-in shower enclosure including tray and screen fitting: from £1,600.

## Tiling only

Wall or floor tiling: £55 per square metre. Minimum charge £300. Removal of old tiles: £15 per square metre.

## Small jobs

Replacing a toilet, basin or taps: £180 per item. Resealing a bath or shower: £90.
