# Frequently asked questions

## Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Surveys and written quotes are free within 15 miles of our workshop. Further out there is a £40 travel charge, refunded if you go ahead.

## How soon can you start?

We are usually booked 3 to 5 weeks ahead for full refits. Small jobs can often be fitted in within a week.

## Do you supply the bathroom suite?

You can supply your own or we can order one through our trade account. We do not add a markup on materials we order for you.

## What areas do you cover?

We work within 25 miles of our workshop. Beyond that, ask us and we will see what we can do.

## What guarantee do you give?

All workmanship is guaranteed for 2 years. Products carry the manufacturer's warranty.

## How do I pay?

A 20% deposit secures your start date. The balance is due on completion. We accept bank transfer and card.
